Before you choose

Questions women often ask.

Which tier is right for me?

Seed if you want a written, personalised starting point to work with independently. Bloom if you want to feel the work in your body, not just read it — one private session with Oshikha. Sacred if you want more space: two private sessions to go deeper.

What is the difference between Bloom and Sacred?

Bloom includes one private breathwork and healing session. Sacred includes two. Neither Blueprint offer includes ongoing voice-note support — that lives in the Private Immersion.

What happens after I pay?

You're taken straight to your intake questionnaire. Your answers shape your Blueprint. For Bloom and Sacred, you'll then book your private session(s) straight into Oshikha's calendar — Sacred clients use the same link twice. Blueprint delivery timings are confirmed as part of your booking.

What happens in a private healing session?

A guided one-to-one experience drawing on breathwork and emotional wellbeing practices. The exact approach depends on the session and your agreed intentions. Sessions are not a substitute for medical or mental healthcare.

Can I use the Blueprint alongside fertility treatment?

It can complement your broader wellbeing practices, but it does not replace medical advice or treatment. Discuss changes to diet, supplements, herbs or other health practices with your healthcare team, especially if you're in treatment or taking medication.

Is the Sacred Body Blueprint a fertility treatment?

No. It is a personalised preconception wellbeing and educational resource. It does not diagnose or treat infertility, replace fertility investigations or guarantee conception. Continue working with your fertility specialist or other qualified healthcare professionals for medical assessment and treatment.

Does the Blueprint guarantee pregnancy?

No. Conception depends on many factors, and no Blueprint or wellbeing practice can guarantee an outcome. The intention is a more considered, informed and intentional approach to your preconception preparation.
