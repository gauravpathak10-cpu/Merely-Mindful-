1. Parties & Cohort

This agreement is between Merely Mindful Limited ("we", "us") and you, the participant named below, regarding your enrolment in the 3-Month Conscious Conception Programme, September Cohort.

This programme is run as a small, live cohort. Your enrolment holds a place that could otherwise go to someone else, so we ask for a genuine commitment to attend live sessions where possible. The Programme fee is €2,499. A payment plan is available on request; if you are on a payment plan, the agreed schedule remains due in full regardless of attendance, except where the Programme itself is cancelled or rescheduled by us.

Our full cancellation and refund terms — including your statutory rights under EU consumer law — are set out in our Refund & Cancellation Policy, which forms part of this agreement.

5. Not a Substitute for Medical Care

This Programme offers a holistic, educational approach to conscious conception and fertility preparation. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. We do not diagnose, treat, or claim to cure any medical or psychological condition. You agree to continue consulting your own healthcare provider regarding any medical concerns throughout the Programme, and to inform us of anything relevant to your safe participation.

Results are not guaranteed. Your outcomes depend on your own circumstances, participation, and many factors outside our control.

6. Confidentiality

Because this Programme involves a small group sharing personal experiences, we ask all participants to treat what is shared by others in the group as confidential, and not to repeat or discuss it outside the group without permission. We extend the same respect to what you share with us. See our Privacy Policy for how we handle your personal data.

7. Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of Ireland. Nothing in this agreement affects your statutory rights as a consumer under EU and Irish law.
