// Healing Tonight's Ritual

Daily Ritual Container

Build a 3-part ritual: morning (before the phone), midday (one breath reset), evening (a closing signal to the body that the day is done).

// Homework & Reflection Between Sessions
Practical task

Design your 3-part daily ritual and try it for 3 days straight.

1. Where in my day does my body brace, even when nothing is actually wrong?
2. What is one ritual from my past that made me feel safe?
3. What would a nervous system that felt held all day actually do differently?
4. What is the smallest version of a daily ritual I will actually keep?

“You cannot think your way into safety. You practice your way there, one regulated breath at a time.”
