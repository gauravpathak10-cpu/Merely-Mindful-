// Teaching What We're Exploring
Personal

Conscious conception means meeting pregnancy with intention, not just biology — aligning body, mind, and spirit before you try.

Science

The 90 days before conception — the Zero Trimester — shape the quality of the egg and sperm that will become your child.

Science

Epigenetics shows diet, stress, sleep, and environment in this window can change how genes are expressed, without changing the DNA sequence itself.

Science

Every egg you will ever release already existed inside you while you were a fetus in your mother's womb — you were alive inside her before you were born.

Personal

That means three generations are already part of this conception: your grandmother's body, your mother's womb, and your own.

Spiritual

Many traditions hold that before physical conception, you can consciously call in the soul of your child — an invitation, not a guarantee.

// Homework & Reflection Between Sessions
Practical task

Write your Inner Child Letter (unedited, to yourself at age 7) and your Sankalp — one firm, positive declaration of intention for this conception journey. Keep both somewhere you'll see them daily.

1. What does 'conscious conception' mean to me, beyond the biology of it?
2. If my egg has existed since I was in my mother's womb, what have I carried in it — knowingly or not?
3. What do I want the 90 days before conception to give my future child, that wasn't given to me?
4. What am I ready to call in?
