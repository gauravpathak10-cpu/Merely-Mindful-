Privacy notice

You trust us with personal and often tender parts of your story. This page explains, in plain words, what we collect, why, who helps us, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Merely Mindful Limited is a company registered in Ireland, founded by Oshikha Pandey. We're the data controller for the information described here.

For anything about your information, email oshika@merelymindful.com.

What we collect

  • When you buy something: your name, email address, phone number and billing country, and whether you'd like WhatsApp updates. Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see your card details.
  • Your Sacred Body Blueprint intake: the answers you choose to share. These can include health information, such as your cycle, your fertility journey, diagnoses, treatments, losses, digestion, sleep, stress and emotional wellbeing.
  • Sessions and messages: booking details when you schedule a session, and the emails and WhatsApp messages we exchange.
  • Masterclass, assessment, programme applications and newsletter: your name, email address and the answers you give.
  • The Circle portal: your account details and what you choose to save there.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To deliver what you've bought, including your Blueprint, sessions and the messages that go with them. This is necessary for our contract with you.
  • Your health information is used only with your explicit consent, which you give on the intake form. You can withdraw it at any time by emailing us. If you do, we won't be able to prepare or continue your Blueprint.
  • WhatsApp updates are sent only if you opt in. Reply STOP at any time to stop them.
  • Our newsletter is sent only if you subscribe, and every issue has an unsubscribe link.
  • Accounting and legal duties, such as keeping payment records.

How your Blueprint is prepared

We use AI writing tools (Claude, made by Anthropic) to help draft your Blueprint from your intake answers. Oshikha then reads, edits and approves every Blueprint herself before it's sent to you. No decision about you is made by automated means alone.

Only your first name and your wellbeing answers are shared for drafting, never your email address, phone number, surname or Instagram. We've switched off the setting that would allow Anthropic to use our conversations to train its AI models.

Who helps us

We use trusted service providers who process information on our behalf and only as we instruct:

  • Stripe: payments.
  • Google: stores intake answers and Blueprints (Google Sheets and Drive) and runs our automation.
  • Anthropic: AI drafting support, as described above.
  • Kit: sends the emails about your Blueprint, and our newsletter if you subscribe.
  • Meta (WhatsApp): WhatsApp updates, only if you opt in.
  • Calendly: session booking.
  • Supabase: The Circle portal.
  • GitHub: hosts this website. Google Fonts displays our typefaces, so your browser connects to Google when pages load.

We never sell your information.

Transfers outside the EU

Some of these providers are based outside the European Economic Area, mainly in the United States. Where that happens, transfers are protected by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Intake answers and your Blueprint: 24 months after your Blueprint is delivered, so Oshikha can support any follow-up. They're then deleted. You can ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Payment records: 6 years, as Irish tax law requires.
  • Newsletter details: until you unsubscribe.

Keeping it safe

We keep access to a minimum. Only Oshikha and the person who looks after our systems can see your intake answers. Your Blueprint is sent to you as a private download link that only people with the link can open, and it closes automatically after 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, limit how we use it, or send you a copy. You can also object to how we use it and withdraw your consent at any time. Just email oshika@merelymindful.com and we'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.

Age

Our services are for adults aged 18 and over.

Changes

If we change how we handle your information, we'll update this page and the date below.
